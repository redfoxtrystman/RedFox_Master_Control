================================================================
POSSIBLE FUTURE SYSTEM: PKVAULT EXPEDITIONS / IDLE RPG COMPANION
================================================================

Core idea
---------
A separate companion application linked to PKVault rather than a large feature embedded directly into PKVault.

PKVault remains the authoritative owner of:
- Pokemon
- Pokemon identity / UUID
- inventory
- money
- quests
- achievements
- contracts
- permanent collection state

The companion app would handle:
- expedition boards
- expedition timers
- expedition health
- fainting
- healing / Pokemon Center
- expedition danger
- expedition-specific status
- expedition results presentation

The companion app should never directly edit PKVault's SQLite database, .item files, Pokemon files, or game saves behind PKVault's back.

Pokemon checkout / locking
--------------------------
When a Pokemon is sent on an expedition:
- It must be physically stored in PKVault first.
- PKVault marks that Pokemon as Away / On Expedition.
- The Pokemon cannot simultaneously be moved to a game, traded, deleted, or sent on another expedition.
- The companion app receives the Pokemon's relevant snapshot/data through a dedicated PKVault companion API.
- When the expedition ends, the companion app returns the result through PKVault.
- PKVault then applies approved EXP / level progression, loot, and unlocks the Pokemon.

PKVault may show a small status badge such as:
  Away - Deep Trench Expedition - 2h 17m remaining

But the actual game UI belongs in the companion app.

Expedition environments
-----------------------
Potential expedition/environment types include:
- Land
- Air
- Sea
- Underwater
- Forest
- Cave
- Mountain
- Volcanic
- Frozen
- Ruins
- Desert
- Swamp
- Special / Mythic locations such as Distortion-themed ruins, Ultra-Space-like zones, ancient temples, deep trenches, etc.

Different Pokemon should perform better in environments that fit their:
- type
- level
- ability
- stats
- moves
- team composition

Environment matches should improve expedition performance rather than always being absolute requirements.

Example:
A high-level Tyranitar may still brute-force a moderate Forest expedition even if it is not the ideal type.

Expedition rating
-----------------
Possible calculation:
Pokemon level
+ environment affinity
+ team synergy
+ ability bonuses
+ special bonuses
= Expedition Power

Example expedition:
Deep Sea Salvage
Duration: 4 hours
Recommended level: 45+
Team size: 3
Favored: Water / Ice
Expedition Power: 184 / 150
Result estimate: Excellent

Potential result tiers:
- Barely Successful
- Successful
- Great
- Excellent
- Perfect

Higher ratings can increase:
- loot rolls
- rare-loot chance
- EXP
- survival / health outcomes

Daily expedition board
----------------------
The companion app could generate multiple expeditions each day with different:
- environments
- durations
- difficulty levels
- recommended levels
- party-size requirements
- favored types / abilities
- loot pools

Example daily board:
- Forest Foraging - 30m - Easy
- Coastal Search - 1h - Easy
- Underground Survey - 2h - Medium
- Mountain Air Patrol - 2h - Medium
- Volcanic Excavation - 4h - Hard
- Deep Trench Recovery - 6h - Hard
- Ancient Ruins - 8h - Elite
- Mysterious Distortion - 12h - Mythic

Potential duration bands:
- Quick: 15-30 minutes
- Standard: 1-2 hours
- Long: 4-6 hours
- Overnight: 8-12 hours
- Epic / Mythic: 12-24 hours

Expedition parties
------------------
Potentially limit simultaneous parties.

Example progression:
- Start with 1 active expedition party.
- Unlock additional party slots later through achievements, progression, or Shop upgrades.
- A later maximum could be 2-3 concurrent parties.

Health system
-------------
If Expeditions is ever built, persistent expedition health should be part of it.

Pokemon can return at different HP percentages based on:
- expedition danger
- team strength
- environment match
- encounters / events
- success rating

Example return:
- Gyarados - 64% HP
- Lapras - 31% HP
- Vaporeon - 78% HP

Important:
Pokemon should NEVER permanently die.

At 0 HP:
- Pokemon is fainted.
- It returns from the expedition.
- It cannot be redeployed until revived / healed.

The expedition can potentially end early if the team becomes unable to continue.

Example:
Emergency Retreat
Team HP became too low.
Expedition ended early.
Partial rewards recovered.

Pokemon Center
--------------
The companion app could include a Pokemon Center.

Potential rule:
- Pokemon Center healing is free.
- Healing may take some real time as part of the idle-game loop.

PKVault healing items could optionally be consumed to heal instantly.

Potential item behavior:
- Potion: +20 expedition HP
- Super Potion: +60
- Hyper Potion: +120
- Max Potion: full heal
- Revive: revive a fainted Pokemon to 50%
- Max Revive: fully revive

If items are consumed, the companion app must request the transaction through PKVault's API so PKVault remains authoritative.

Keep RPG stats small
--------------------
Do not turn this into an excessive spreadsheet RPG.

Initial expedition-specific state should probably be limited to:
- HP
- Level / EXP
- Fainted state

Potential later addition:
- Fatigue

Avoid unnecessary systems unless proven useful:
- hunger
- morale
- injury tables
- equipment durability
- many extra meters

The Pokemon's existing canonical data already supplies most of the RPG depth:
- species
- form
- level
- stats
- typing
- ability
- moves
- nature
- held item

EXP and leveling
----------------
Expeditions should award real experience rather than simply applying "+2 levels."

Lower-level Pokemon should gain levels more quickly.
High-level Pokemon should require substantially more expedition EXP.

Pokemon should not automatically evolve while away.

If a Pokemon reaches an evolution requirement:
- it returns with the new level / EXP
- normal evolution decisions remain outside the expedition system

Level-up move handling must be designed deliberately.
The companion app should not blindly overwrite a player's moveset.

Abilities / Pokemon traits
--------------------------
Pokemon properties could influence expedition performance.

Examples:
- Flying types: Air bonus
- Water types: Sea / Underwater bonus
- Pickup: extra item roll
- Illuminate: Cave / Underwater exploration bonus
- Flame Body: Frozen-area bonus
- Levitate: Air / Ruins bonus
- Sturdy / Rock Head: Mountain / Volcanic bonus

Do not attempt hundreds of special interactions in the first version.

- Great Balls

- Potions
- Super Potions
- Hyper Potions
- Full Restores

- berries

- held items

Rare Discovery!

Contracts integration
---------------------
Future PKVault Contracts could use Expedition objectives.

Examples:
Master Explorer - Gold Contract
Complete 5 Hard expeditions with an Excellent rating or better.

Into the Abyss - Mythic Contract
Complete a Mythic Underwater expedition using three level 80+ Water Pokemon.

This would allow Contracts to cover more than catches or Dex work.

Persistence rules
-----------------
Automatic expedition rewards must follow the same principle as automatic quest rewards:
- result is validated
- reward is committed immediately
- loot is persisted immediately
- legitimate EXP changes are persisted immediately
- Pokemon lock is released only after successful result application

Manual PKVault edits remain separate and should not accidentally be committed by an expedition reward.

Companion API concept
---------------------
PKVault should expose a controlled API rather than sharing databases.

Possible operations:
- Get available Pokemon
- Check out / lock Pokemon for expedition
- Read allowed Pokemon snapshot
- Consume PKVault item
- Grant expedition loot
- Apply validated EXP / level changes
- Return Pokemon
- Release expedition lock
- Query active expedition locks

The API should be generic enough that other PKVault companion applications could theoretically use it later.

Why separate app
----------------
Once persistent health, fainting, Pokemon Center healing, timers, expedition danger, events, team building, loot, and progression are involved, Expeditions becomes a game rather than merely a PKVault storage feature.

Keeping it separate:
- keeps PKVault focused
- reduces risk to storage/save functionality
- makes Expeditions optional
- lets the idle-RPG grow without bloating PKVault
- keeps Pokemon/inventory authority in one place
