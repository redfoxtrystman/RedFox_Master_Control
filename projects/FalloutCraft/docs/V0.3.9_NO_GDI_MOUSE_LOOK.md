# FalloutCraft v0.3.9 — no-GDI + mouse-look test

v0.3.8 established the stable baseline: disabling the temporary GDI compositor stops the Fallout freeze, and Minecraft-style movement works.

v0.3.9 keeps that no-GDI freeze fix and keeps `Actor::SetPosition(..., false)`, but restores the Fallout look-routing call that v0.3.7 disabled. This isolates whether keyboard/mouse look can be restored without reintroducing the compositor freeze.

The Minecraft HUD remains intentionally absent in this binary test. The replacement HUD path is the source-based D3D11 Present renderer under `source/dx11_overlay`.
