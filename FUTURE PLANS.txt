PKVAULT - FUTURE PLANS
======================

Purpose
-------
This file records planned PKVault features that are intentionally NOT implemented yet.
It is the design source of truth for the future Shop, Daily Quest, Quest, and Achievement systems.

CURRENT IMPLEMENTATION STATUS - ALPHA43
---------------------------------------
- Legendary first-catch achievements are live and permanently claimed across PKVault.
- Completed Daily Quest / Quest / Achievement cards turn green.
- Permanent caught-species, Pokédex, playtime, Legendary, and reward history persists even after the originating save is removed.
- Generated PKVault sample save is excluded from all quest, achievement, and daily progress.
- Saved Pokémon fingerprint and playtime baselines are implemented for live daily activity detection.
- Daily quest pool now includes catch-count, different-species, new-Dex-entry, and short-playtime objectives.
- Daily rerolls are live: 1 FREE reroll each local day, then paid rerolls from the PKVault Pokédollar Bank.
- Paid rerolls use the implemented escalating curve: ₽2,500, ₽5,000, ₽7,500, ... increasing by ₽2,500 and capped at ₽50,000 per reroll until the next daily reset.
- Playtime quest ladder is live per real loaded game/save profile: 15 minutes -> ₽2,000; 2 hours -> ₽10,000; 10 hours -> ₽25,000.
- Game-aware Pokédex quests are live at 25 / 50 / 100 caught, 75% completion, and full applicable Pokédex completion.
- Full-Dex target reward is live: 1 Master Ball + 15 Ultra Balls + 10 Potions.
- Existing permanent catch milestones and Legendary achievements remain one-time and cannot be farmed by removing/re-adding saves.
- Alpha43 adds Mythical first-catch rewards, rebalanced Legendary rewards, Shiny counts/species counts, evolution tracking, training dailies, longer playtime ladders, regional-origin Pokédex collections, starter sets, Eeveelutions, and Legendary-group collections.
- Remaining expansion areas include richer game-specific/cross-game objectives, type/species hunts, conditional activities where verifiable, voucher quests tied to the future Shop, and further balance passes.

Core design rule:
PKVault should add a cross-game progression layer without turning supported Pokémon games
into ROM hacks and without making their economies trivial.


============================================================
1. PKVAULT SHOP / SHARED ECONOMY
============================================================

PKVault already has:
- Shared Pokédollar Bank.
- Cross-save item storage.
- Game/profile-aware item compatibility.

Future plan:
Add a PKVault Shop where items can be bought and sold using Pokédollars.

Canonical pricing
-----------------
PKVault must use ONE canonical price table.

Example:
If Poké Balls are ₽200 in the PKVault price table, they cost ₽200 regardless of whether
the destination is Pokémon Red, Silver, Uranium, Insurgence, or another supported game.

The selected game/save controls:
- Whether an item is compatible with that game.
- Whether the item can be delivered to that save.
- Which game-specific items are shown.

The selected game/save does NOT change the shared PKVault price of a common item.

This prevents cross-game buy-low/sell-high exploits.

Fangame-only items may receive their own canonical PKVault prices when no official shared
item equivalent exists.

Potential Shop features:
- Buy.
- Sell.
- Quantity selector.
- MAX purchase based on money and destination capacity.
- Shopping cart / bulk purchases.
- Search and item-category filters.
- Pay from PKVault Bank or a selected game's wallet.
- Send sale proceeds to PKVault Bank or a selected game's wallet.
- Sell duplicates while keeping a user-selected minimum quantity.
- Per-game compatibility filtering.
- Never offer an item to a destination that cannot safely represent/store it.


============================================================
2. THREE DISTINCT PROGRESSION SYSTEMS
============================================================

The UI should eventually separate:

DAILY QUESTS | QUESTS | ACHIEVEMENTS

Daily Quests
------------
Small repeatable tasks that refresh each day.

They should:
- Be short.
- Be reliably detectable.
- Give modest rewards.
- Avoid unique/rare accomplishments better suited to Achievements.
- Only generate objectives compatible with currently loaded/recognized saves.

Quests
------
Permanent progression tracks organized into categories.

Candidate categories:
- Playtime.
- Pokédex.
- Catching.
- Evolution.
- Legendary / Mythical.
- Shiny.
- Collection.
- Game-Specific.
- Cross-Game / PKVault.

Achievements
------------
Permanent one-time accomplishments.

Achievements can be larger, rarer, and more rewarding than dailies.
They must never become repeatable reward farms.


============================================================
3. SAVE-BASED TRACKING - NO ROM HACKS REQUIRED
============================================================

PKVault should track progression externally.

For older games, PKVault does NOT need to patch or modify ROMs.

Basic approach:
1. Snapshot a supported save.
2. Record stable Pokémon fingerprints and relevant save statistics.
3. Watch/reload the save when it changes.
4. Compare the new save against PKVault's previous snapshot.
5. Convert verified changes into quest events.
6. Exclude known PKVault transfers/imports from "caught" progress.
7. Update PKVault's own permanent quest/event ledger.

Gen 1 identity is less rich than later generations, so use a stable external fingerprint
based on available Pokémon data such as:
- OT Trainer ID.
- OT name.
- Species.
- DVs.
- Other stable data that survives normal gameplay.

Gen 3+ has stronger identity/origin data and can be tracked more confidently.

Important limitation:
If a Pokémon is caught and then released before any save state visible to PKVault ever
contains it, a save-only tracker cannot know that catch occurred.

Optional future emulator integration could improve live detection without modifying ROMs,
but the base quest system must work from normal save files.


============================================================
4. PLAYTIME QUESTS
============================================================

Use each game's stored playtime and compare it against PKVault's previous snapshot.

Current intended early balance:

Pokémon Red example progression:
- Play 15 minutes -> ₽2,000.
- Play 2 hours -> ₽10,000.
- Play 10 hours -> ₽25,000.

Later playtime rewards should rise slowly and must not trivialize the games.

General balance rule:
Do NOT hand out huge cash rewards simply for leaving a save running.
PKVault progression should feel useful, not like an infinite-money cheat.


============================================================
5. POKÉDEX QUESTS
============================================================

Pokédex quests are permanent milestone quests.

Example milestone ladder:
- 25 caught -> small Ball reward.
- 50 caught -> modest Great Ball reward.
- 100 caught -> modest Ultra Ball reward.
- 75% complete -> larger but still reasonable item bundle.
- Complete the game's full applicable Pokédex -> major completion reward.

Current full-Dex reward target:
- 1 Master Ball.
- 15 Ultra Balls.
- 10 Potions.

A game's completion target must be game-aware.
Pokémon Red, Uranium, Insurgence, and later official games do not necessarily have the
same applicable Dex requirements.


============================================================
6. LEGENDARY ACHIEVEMENTS
============================================================

Each unique Legendary should have a permanent first-catch achievement.

Example:
- First verified Mewtwo catch -> one-time Master Ball reward.
- First verified Articuno catch -> its own one-time reward.
- First verified Lugia catch -> its own one-time reward.

A specific Legendary may only trigger its unique reward ONCE across PKVault.

Moving the same Legendary between saves, withdrawing/redepositing it, or importing a
Pokémon PKVault already knows must NOT retrigger the achievement.

PKVault should maintain a permanent claimed ledger per qualifying species.


============================================================
7. DAILY QUEST PHILOSOPHY
============================================================

Dailies should NOT duplicate permanent achievements.

Good daily territory:
- Ordinary catches.
- Short playtime.
- Common type/species targets.
- Small cross-game activity goals.
- Small inventory/economy tasks where PKVault can exclude its own transfers.
- Variety goals using only games currently available to the user.

Bad daily territory:
- First shiny.
- First Legendary.
- Catch Mewtwo.
- Complete a Pokédex.
- Own every starter.
- Long lifetime playtime milestones.
- "Old Save Day" / "play a save you have not used recently" objectives.
- "Game Hopper" / "make progress in several different saves today" objectives.
- Any other unique collection milestone.

Those belong to Quests or Achievements.

Daily quest candidate pool
--------------------------
Examples that fit the repeatable daily system without colliding with permanent achievements:
- Catch 3 Pokémon.
- Catch 5 Pokémon.
- Catch 3 different species.
- Catch 3 Pokémon of a generated common type.
- Catch 2 of a generated common species.
- Catch 5 of a generated common species.
- Add 1 new species to a save's Dex.
- Add 3 new species to a save's Dex.
- Gain 5 total Pokémon levels.
- Gain 10 total Pokémon levels.
- Train one Pokémon by 3 levels.
- Evolve 1 Pokémon.
- Play one supported game for 15 minutes.
- Play one supported game for 30 minutes.
- Play two supported games for 10 minutes each.
- Catch a Pokémon in two different supported games.
- Game Focus: catch 3 Pokémon in a specific loaded game.
- Uranium-specific: catch 2 Uranium-original species, only when the save makes this reasonable.
- Insurgence-specific: catch 3 Pokémon in Insurgence.
- Variety Hunt: catch one Pokémon of three different common types.
- No Repeats: catch 5 Pokémon, all different species.
- Regional Catch: catch 3 Pokémon from a generated supported region.
- Fishing: fish X times, but ONLY for games/integrations where PKVault can reliably
  detect fishing attempts. Do not enable this from save-only tracking when the save
  format does not preserve a fishing counter/event history.

Conditional activity dailies
----------------------------
Some activity quests are good ideas but cannot be universally verified from a normal
save file.

Fishing is the first example:
- "Fish 5 times."
- "Fish 10 times."
- "Catch 3 Pokémon while fishing."

Rules:
- Enable a fishing daily only when that game's save data or a supported emulator/live
  integration exposes enough information to verify fishing activity.
- Never guess fishing attempts from the presence of Water-type Pokémon.
- Never infer that a Pokémon was fished solely because that species can be encountered
  with a rod.
- For older games whose save files do not record fishing attempts, leave "Fish X times"
  out of the generated daily pool unless future emulator/live tracking is active.
- A save-only fallback may use a different objective such as "Catch X Water-type
  Pokémon", but it must be presented as a different quest, not as fishing.

Specific-species dailies must avoid:
- Legendary/Mythical Pokémon.
- One-of-a-kind encounters.
- Gift-only/event-only species.
- Starter-only species unless the objective is explicitly designed around a repeatable source.
- Species the player has not plausibly reached.

Prefer selecting ordinary species already marked Seen in one of the relevant saves.


============================================================
8. REWARD BALANCE
============================================================

Daily rewards:
- Hundreds to low-thousands of Pokédollars.
- Small numbers of Poké Balls / Great Balls.
- Basic healing/status items.
- Occasional modest Ultra Ball rewards for harder dailies.
- Avoid Master Balls as ordinary daily rewards.

Daily rerolls:
- The player receives 1 FREE daily quest reroll per daily reset.
- After the free reroll is used, additional rerolls cost Pokédollars.
- Paid reroll cost should be taken from the PKVault Pokédollar Bank.
- The reroll price should be meaningful but not punishing.
- Paid rerolls should not become cheaper than simply completing an easy daily.
- Consider escalating the price for multiple paid rerolls on the same day to discourage
  endlessly rerolling until the easiest possible quest appears.
- Implemented Alpha42 curve: first reroll is FREE; paid rerolls are ₽2,500, ₽5,000,
  ₽7,500, and so on, increasing by ₽2,500 per paid reroll and capped at ₽50,000
  per reroll until the next local daily reset.

Permanent quest rewards:
- Larger but controlled cash.
- Better item bundles.
- Progression-appropriate Balls and healing items.

Major achievements:
- Unique/premium rewards can be appropriate.
- Master Balls should remain special.
- Mythical first-catch achievements award Master Balls.
- Most individual Legendary first-catch rewards should instead be 1-3 Nuggets or
  roughly 25-50 Ultra Balls depending on the Legendary's tier.
- Full-Dex / full-region completion can still justify Master Balls because those are
  major collection achievements.
- Aggregate Legendary milestones must scale above ordinary catch milestones; catching
  3 unique Legendaries should never reward less than an easier 50-species milestone.

Core rule:
PKVault should reward playing Pokémon, not remove the need to play Pokémon.

Quest game-availability indicators
----------------------------------
Every Daily Quest, Quest, and Achievement should show which supported game(s) can
actually satisfy that objective.

UI concept:
- Show one or more small game icons/badges on the quest card.
- Hovering an icon/group opens a tooltip listing the compatible games by name.
- If several games share the same objective, group them compactly instead of filling
  the card with text.
- The tooltip should use full names, for example:
  "Pokémon X"
  "Pokémon Y"
- A combined quest that works in both can show the X/Y game badges and a hover tooltip:
  "Can be completed in Pokémon X or Pokémon Y."
- Fangames should use their own recognizable badges/icons:
  Pokémon Uranium, Pokémon Insurgence, etc.
- Cross-game quests should explicitly say that progress may be combined across the
  listed compatible games when that is actually allowed.
- Game-specific quests should show only the relevant game.
- A quest must never display a game badge unless PKVault's tracker can actually verify
  that objective in that game.
- Conditional activities such as fishing may therefore show fewer compatible games
  than a generic catch quest.

The game indicator is informational only; it does not change canonical shop pricing or
reward values.

Possible Daily card:
Catch 5 Pidgey
[Red] [Blue] [Yellow]
Progress: 3 / 5
Reward: 10 Poké Balls
Expires: next daily reset

Possible permanent quest card:
Kanto Traveler
Play Pokémon Red for 2 hours
Progress: 1h 26m / 2h
Reward: ₽10,000


============================================================
10. IMPORTANT IMPLEMENTATION RULES
============================================================

- Never require ROM modification for the base quest/achievement system.
- PKVault must keep its own event/history ledger.
- PKVault-originated transfers must not count as catches.
- Re-moving the same Pokémon between saves must not farm progress.
- Daily generation must respect the user's actually loaded/supported games.
- Do not generate Insurgence-only dailies without an Insurgence save.
- Do not generate Uranium-only dailies without a Uranium save.
- Reward delivery should normally go to the PKVault item/money banks, allowing the user
  to decide which compatible save receives the reward.
- Shared shop prices must stay canonical across games.


Quest vouchers / contracts
---------------------------
The future PKVault Shop should sell optional extremely difficult quest vouchers. Buying
a voucher adds a hard, persistent contract to the Quest page; it is not a normal daily
and should not expire at the next reset.

Core rules:
- The voucher itself costs Pokédollars from the shared PKVault Pokédollar Bank.
- No free rerolls. Once purchased, the objective is fixed until completed or explicitly
  abandoned.
- Abandoning a voucher does NOT refund the purchase price.
- Voucher quests must use the same anti-farming history/identity rules as normal quests.
- Never generate a voucher objective PKVault cannot reliably verify.
- Voucher rewards should be materially better than normal quest rewards because the
  player paid for access and the objective is intentionally difficult.
- Voucher rewards may be randomized from a tier-specific reward pool, but the UI must
  show the exact reward bundle before the player purchases the voucher.
- Avoid infinite-profit loops where a trivially repeatable voucher can be bought and
  completed for more liquid value than its cost with no meaningful gameplay.

Example starter voucher bundle:
- 10 Potions.
- 20 Poké Balls.
- 10 Great Balls.
- 5 Ultra Balls.
- Additional random high-value items can be added by harder tiers.

Suggested tiers:
- Bronze Contract: difficult single-game objective, moderate mixed item bundle.
- Silver Contract: longer or multi-condition objective, stronger Balls/healing/Rare Candy.
- Gold Contract: very difficult collection/training objective, premium bundle with
  Nuggets, Ultra Balls, Rare Candies, Full Restores, etc.
- Mythic Contract: exceptional late-game objective; Master Balls may appear here, but
  should remain rare and the objective must justify them.

Possible voucher objectives:
- Catch/register a large number of new species after purchase.
- Catch several different types or species from a generated eligible pool.
- Gain a large number of total levels across tracked Pokémon.
- Evolve multiple tracked Pokémon.
- Complete a major percentage of a game-specific Pokédex after purchase.
- Register multiple new Legendary species.
- Register multiple new Shiny Pokémon.
- Complete several different supported objectives in one contract.
- Cross-game contracts only when every required game is actually loaded and verifiable.
- Preserve save-specific money/item capacity limits when withdrawing rewards.
- Keep the economy intentionally conservative.
- Never generate Old Save Day or Game Hopper daily objectives.
- Allow exactly one free daily reroll per reset; further rerolls consume Pokédollars.
- Paid rerolls should debit the PKVault Pokédollar Bank and may use an escalating same-day cost.
