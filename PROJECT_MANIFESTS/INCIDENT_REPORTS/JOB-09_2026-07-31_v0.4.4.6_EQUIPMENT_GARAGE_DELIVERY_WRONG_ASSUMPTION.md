# JOB-09 v0.4.4.6 Incident — Equipment Garage Delivery Wrong Assumption

**Date:** 2026-07-31  
**Owner:** David / Captain  
**Affected build:** JOB-09 v0.4.4.6  
**Status:** CONFIRMED DESIGN MISTAKE — CORRECTION REQUIRED

## Owner correction

David confirmed that `FP Crane Chains 2 rotatable chains` is a spawnable BeamNG item and BeamNG treats it through the vehicle/Career inventory system, just like the walking-character configuration and other spawnable objects. These records may legitimately be stored in an RLS/Career garage.

## Incorrect v0.4.4.6 behavior

v0.4.4.6 added a real-world title/equipment classification gate to native garage delivery. The gate blocked chains, attachments, walkers, props and other spawnable configurations when their catalog role was equipment/non-road.

That assumption was wrong for BeamNG gameplay. Garage-delivery eligibility must follow BeamNG's actual inventory behavior, not real-world vehicle-title semantics.

## Required correction

1. Allow any exact spawnable model/config record that Career/RLS accepts through `career_modules_inventory.addVehicle` to be delivered into a linked RLS garage.
2. Preserve exact model/config identity and existing transaction/idempotency protections.
3. Do not use abandoned/lien eligibility or equipment-role classification as a garage-delivery gate.
4. Keep abandoned-call generation rules separate: emergency vehicles and equipment may still be excluded from inappropriate abandoned/private-lien call selection without blocking valid garage storage.
5. Replace `native/titled vehicle` UI language with `BeamNG/Career inventory item` language where appropriate.
6. Preserve v0.4.4.6 as rollback and build a focused v0.4.4.7 correction.

## Change authorization

David explicitly corrected the assumption and requested the mistake be fixed. No Browser Core, JOB-04, JOB-13, Random Events or stock Career/RLS files are authorized for modification.
