## Exact paid-RLS source findings relevant to job queues

### Native Repo is singleton
`gameplay/repo.lua` stores one module-global `repoJobInstance` and `M.getRepoJobInstance()` always returns that same instance. It also uses shared/module-global route/delivery marker behavior.

Therefore simply deleting JOB-09's Repo guard and calling `gameplay_repo.generateJob()` again would reset/replace the existing native Repo. That is not a safe multiple-Repo implementation.

Safe immediate policy:
- unrelated RedFox jobs may coexist with an active Repo;
- additional Repo requests can be accepted into a RedFox queue, but only one native Repo may be activated at a time until a deliberate multi-Repo compatibility layer is implemented and tested.

Longer-term requested behavior:
- a Dispatch Queue / Active Jobs list capable of holding multiple accepted towing/recovery assignments;
- where the underlying system supports it, multiple targets may be active and routed in the same trip;
- Repo multi-active support requires deeper compatibility work because native RLS Repo is singleton.

### Native Off-Road Recovery already supports multiples
`gameplay/offroadRecovery.lua` defines:
- `MAX_OFFERS = 3`
- `MAX_ACTIVE_PER_MAP = 3`

It persists multiple active jobs per map and has tracked-job selection.

This should be exposed through RedFox Dispatch instead of reimplemented.

## Repo payout display finding

Native Repo does not persist a fixed payout quote when the target is generated. Its `calculateReward()` uses vehicle value, route distance, Recovery tier multiplier, economy/difficulty settings, and elapsed completion time. Therefore the exact final payment can change during the job.

Required JOB-09 UI behavior:
- do not claim a guaranteed exact payout when RLS has not fixed one;
- show a clearly labeled `Estimated payout` / live estimate once enough job data exists;
- show Repo tier and Recovery payout bonus in player-facing language;
- after completion, show the exact RLS-paid amount if it remains available in the Repo state/history.

Do not expose `reward owner`, `no duplicate`, or JOB-09 internal dedupe language in normal player UI.

## Yard interoperability requirement added

User owns the RLS Recovery Yard and has designated it as an additional RedFox Tow Yard because the native Recovery Yard is geographically remote.

Required behavior:
1. RedFox recovery/tow jobs may be delivered to ANY active RedFox Tow Yard on the current map.
2. Native RLS Off-Road Recovery should be able to use a designated RedFox Tow Yard as a valid recovery destination where compatibility can safely supply a recovery delivery point.
3. The native RLS Recovery Yard, when purchased/designated, must remain usable as a RedFox Tow Yard.
4. No yard should replace another yard; all purchased/designated yards coexist.
5. Player should be able to select destination yard for a job, with sensible nearest-yard/default routing.

Native RLS Off-Road Recovery already has a yard-choice workflow, but its source currently enumerates purchased garages flagged `isRecoveryYard == true` with a `recoveryDropoffSpotName`. JOB-09 must bridge designated RedFox yards deliberately rather than pretending every garage is a native Recovery Yard.

### D. Build the formal RLS Tow business type
Direct paid-RLS source confirms the business framework expects:
- a registered business type via `career_modules_business_businessManager.registerBusiness(...)`;
- registered callbacks via `registerBusinessCallback(...)`;
- a real freeroam facility of that business type;
- that facility must contain `businessGarageId`;
- a matching facility of type `businessGarage` with `sitesFile`, `parkingSpotNames`, zones/capacity;
- purchase/ownership through Business Manager;
- Business Manager then creates the real RLS business bank account.

The existing tuning shop/racing team facilities are the architecture reference. Do NOT restore v0.5.0.22's shortcut that treated `redfoxTow` as if it were already a formally registered business.

### E. Business vehicle lifecycle validation
Before enabling Tow fleet Pull Out / Put Away again:
- create stable RLS business vehicle IDs;
- migrate/attach RedFox company fleet records without duplication;
- verify `businessInventory.pullOutVehicle()` against the Tow business type and its real `businessGarageId`;
- verify parking spot assignment/capacity;
- verify `putAwayVehicle()` persists live part conditions;
- test missing/ghost vehicle recovery;
- preserve config, paint, mileage, part conditions, maintenance state and tire state;
- verify service/engine-swap/customization behavior for business vehicles before advertising those features;
- add transaction/rollback logging around migration and pull/put-away.

### F. Business money integration
After formal Tow business registration:
- use the RLS business bank account as Tow-company money authority;
- towing revenue intended for the company should go to that account where appropriate;
- business expenses/maintenance should use the same account;
- later FoxNet auction proceeds for company/custody assets should settle into the Tow business account according to asset ownership/legal rules;
- remove/retire any redundant fake Tow-company wallet only after migration is proven safe.

## Important architecture rule

Do not confuse `multiple jobs in the RedFox Dispatch Queue` with `multiple native Repo instances`.

The first can and should be implemented now. The second is not supported by RLS 2.7.0.1's current singleton Repo design and requires a separate verified compatibility project rather than a blind guard removal.
