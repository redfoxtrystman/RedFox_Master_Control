## Source-first findings

1. RLS `freeroam_facilities.getFacilitiesByType` signature is `(type, levelName)`. v0.5.0.28 still attempted the reversed fallback `(levelId, "garage")`, which explains the repeated `Tried to get facilities of type "west_coast_usa"` log errors. v0.5.0.29 removes only that invalid reversed attempt.
2. RLS Business Inventory supports persistent business vehicles through `storeVehicle`, `getBusinessVehicles`, `getVehicleById`, `pullOutVehicle`, `putAwayVehicle`, `getPulledOutVehicles`, `updateVehicle`, and `saveBusinessVehicles`.
3. RLS Business Inventory resolves a business facility's `businessGarageId`, then finds a `businessGarage` facility with `sitesFile` and `parkingSpotNames`. Pull Out spawns the stored business vehicle and teleports it to an available garage parking spot. Put Away explicitly requests live `getPartConditions`, saves that snapshot, removes the live vehicle object, and persists the business inventory.
4. Parsed RLS purchased garages receive a resolved `sitesFile` from the facilities loader. The primary RedFox Tow Yard's separately-preserved `rlsGarageId` can therefore safely supply a dynamic businessGarage without editing RLS source files.
5. The generic RLS `BusinessVehiclesTab` exposes sale/tuning/parts lifecycle controls and is not enabled for RedFox Tow in this prototype.
6. The native RLS `BusinessFinancesTab` exposes a Sell Business control. v0.5.0.29 therefore adds a narrow runtime guard for only `businessType == "redfoxTow"`: sale quote/sale calls are blocked with a warning and all other RLS business types delegate to their original functions. The original functions are restored on extension unload.

## Patch behavior

- New version: `0.5.0.29`.
- Keeps the one formal business identity `redfoxTow` / `redfox_tow_company`.
- Adds one dynamic business garage ID: `redfox_tow_company_garage`.
- The dynamic garage exists only when the current map contains the PRIMARY RedFox business yard and that yard is linked to a real purchased RLS garage with usable parking spots.
- The dynamic business garage reuses the purchased garage's parsed `sitesFile`, parking spots, zones, and bounded capacity. It does not replace or edit the purchased RLS garage.
- RedFox business computer registers only the native RLS Finances tab. Generic Vehicles/Home/Jobs/Tuning tabs remain disabled for RedFox Tow.
- RedFox business sale is blocked during the prototype to prevent orphaning the business account, yard links, or stored vehicles.
- New business-storage test rows use the formal `redfox_tow_company` business ID. Existing older profile-specific test mirrors are not silently migrated; the UI requires removing/recreating an old TEST COPY first.
- Promotion to authoritative Tow business storage is blocked unless the formal RLS business is purchased and the dynamic business garage is ready.
- One explicitly promoted claimed Tow Company Garage vehicle can be pulled out through RLS Business Inventory at the primary business yard and put away again.
- v0.5.0.29 limits the RedFox prototype to one pulled-out business vehicle at a time.
- Put Away uses RLS's own live part-condition persistence path before removing the world vehicle. JOB-09 mirrors the resulting partConditions back into its promoted Tow record after the RLS callback.
- No automatic fleet migration.
- No Random Events bridge, new job types, UI redesign, website work, physical PC/toolbox placement, multi-branch physical business garages, Repo payout changes, Recovery destination bridge, or auction changes.
- No RLS source file is edited or replaced.

## Runtime test limits

This remains a one-vehicle prototype. It proves the path `Tow Company Garage record -> verified RLS business row -> promoted authoritative storage -> Pull Out -> live vehicle -> Put Away with part-condition snapshot -> save/reload -> Pull Out again`.

Do not treat live mileage accumulation or live tuning/config changes while pulled out as proven by this version. Exact RLS 2.7.0.1 `putAwayVehicle` explicitly persists part conditions; this patch does not claim additional live-state capture beyond what was source-verified.
