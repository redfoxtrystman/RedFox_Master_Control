# JOB-09 Audit — v0.5.0.30 RLS Tow Business Garage Visible

Date: 2026-08-20

## User feedback driving this patch

- Keep the RedFox Tow WEUI open while the RLS business computer is open; the WEUI is intentionally the current quick-access Tow control surface.
- Treat the non-working RLS business-computer X/close action as a separate issue rather than hiding the WEUI.
- Activate a real garage/vehicle view for the Tow Company so promoted Tow trucks/trailers/vehicles can be seen and managed like RLS business garage vehicles.
- Preserve the existing paid Tow Yard company-storage upgrades as the authoritative logical storage capacity.

## Exact source-first findings

1. RLS `BusinessVehiclesTab.vue` is the native business Garage inventory view. It displays stored business vehicles and supports Pull Out / Put Away.
2. The same native component always renders a Sell action. RLS `businessComputer.sellVehicle()` delegates to the active business module's `sellVehicle(businessId, vehicleId)` function.
3. Therefore RedFox Tow can expose the native Vehicles tab safely if `career_modules_business_redfoxTow` supplies its own fail-closed `sellVehicle` implementation.
4. RLS Business Inventory already persists the promoted RedFox rows from v0.5.0.29. No new vehicle migration is required to display those rows.
5. JOB-09 already has independent per-yard Company Fleet / Shop Bay capacity upgrades. The dynamic RLS `businessGarage` only needs real parking positions for live Pull Out; physical parking count and logical stored capacity are separate concerns.

## v0.5.0.30 changes

- Registers the native RLS `BusinessVehiclesTab` as **Garage** for business type `redfoxTow`.
- Keeps the native RLS **Finances** tab.
- Feeds the RLS business computer formatted data for actual promoted `redfox_tow_company` Business Inventory rows.
- Supplies stored and pulled-out vehicle lists plus active vehicle identity to the RLS business-computer store.
- Uses RLS/Core vehicle configuration metadata where available to provide vehicle name, year, type, and preview image.
- Blocks native RLS vehicle selling for `redfoxTow`; the Sell button may still be visible in the native RLS component, but the Lua sale operation fails closed and warns the player. RedFox disposition must remain authoritative.
- Keeps the existing RedFox business-sale guard.
- Keeps one pulled-out RedFox business vehicle at a time for this prototype.
- Does not hide/close the Tow WEUI when the business computer opens.
- Does not change Tow Yard creation/linking flow.
- Does not change abandoned-vehicle mileage/condition generation.
- Does not silently migrate pre-existing RedFox Tow Company Garage rows into RLS Business Inventory.
- Does not edit or replace any RLS source file.

## Storage expansion rule preserved

JOB-09 already supports paid independent storage upgrades per Tow Yard. `Company Fleet / Shop Bays` remains the logical RedFox storage-capacity field. The dynamic RLS business garage uses actual parking spots from the linked purchased RLS garage for live vehicle Pull Out. Buying logical storage does not fabricate extra physical parking spots at the source property.
