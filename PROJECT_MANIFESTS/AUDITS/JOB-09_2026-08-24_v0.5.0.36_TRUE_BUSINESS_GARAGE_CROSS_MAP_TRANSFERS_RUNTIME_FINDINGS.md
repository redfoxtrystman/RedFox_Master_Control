## Owner safety direction

- Do **not** auto-migrate the existing Tow fleet.
- Test one expendable trailer or car at a time.
- Personal -> Tow transfer should automatically perform all business linking/storage work; the owner should not need developer-style registration/link steps.
- Tow -> Personal must be supported with no automatic sale/payment.
- Tow Yard -> Tow Yard must be supported, including yards on other maps.
- Cross-map movement is a saved backend assignment/shipping concept. A destination map need not be loaded to transfer the stored record; physical pull-out/retrieve happens at the destination when that map is loaded.

## v36 intended design

v36 attempted to replace v35's personal-garage-backed Tow Company vehicles with true RLS Business Inventory storage for newly transferred vehicles. It added a manual one-vehicle-at-a-time transfer path and intended these actions:

- Personal -> Tow Company true business storage
- Tow Yard -> Tow Yard
- Tow Company -> Personal
- Pull Out / Put Away
- Repair
- Paint
- Parts/Tuning
- custody actions from the RedFox Company Computer
- logical Company Fleet / Shop capacity independent of personal garage capacity

No automatic fleet migration was permitted.

## Important runtime findings from owner testing

The v36 approach is **not accepted as the final Tow garage architecture**.

Owner reported:

1. Vehicle preview images are not consistently sized. Some vehicle images appear huge/full-card while others appear small.
2. Vehicle cards do not behave like the normal BeamNG/RLS garage.
3. Vehicle edit/paint/action buttons still do not work correctly.
4. Tow Yard movement dropdowns/actions do not work correctly.
5. `Search Vehicle` does not work.
6. The Company Garage list visually looks acceptable, but it is not the stock normal-garage presentation/interaction the owner wants.
7. The owner wants stock left-click/right-click behavior and the same option menu/actions used by the in-game Career/RLS garage, with RedFox additions layered on top.
8. The existing Tow Company Garage button that said vehicles/edit/paint/parts had previously opened the player's normal personal garages rather than a working Tow-only garage.

## Final desired Tow garage behavior after Icebox is proven

The Tow garage should behave like the normal in-game/RLS garage rather than imitating it.

Expected stock-style behavior:

- same stock vehicle image/card sizing and presentation;
- left-click/select behavior;
- normal stock action/popover/context behavior;
- Retrieve / Replace current vehicle where appropriate;
- Performance Index;
- Repair;
- Favorite, supporting more than one favorite;
- Paint / Customize;
- Parts / Configuration / Tuning;
- Personalize plate;
- Rename vehicle;
- stock sorting/filtering behavior where practical.

RedFox additions should be additive, not a replacement UI:

- Move to Tow Yard;
- Transfer to Personal Garage;
- cross-map shipping/request;
- yard assignment/status;
- Tow business ownership/accounting;
- custody/legal status where relevant.

## Tow Yard view expectation

The Tow Company garage screen should be able to show **all Tow Yards**, not only the current map's yard.

The owner should be able to sort/filter vehicles by yard and favorites, select multiple favorites, and move stored vehicles between yards.

Longer-term cross-map flow:

1. Player travels to a new map.
2. Player creates a new Tow Yard/shop on that map.
3. From the new shop, player requests the trucks/equipment needed from another Tow Yard/map.
4. Initially this can be an immediate backend garage-to-garage transfer for testing.
5. Later, all cross-map business vehicle transfers should route through a dedicated shipping/delivery location and be picked up there.

Vehicles are saved records, so the architecture must not depend on the source map being loaded merely to reassign/ship a stored vehicle.
