## Fresh JOB-09 findings

### Recovery skill gap

`M.buildTowRewardData()` in v0.5.0.23 currently awards Money + BeamXP + Labourer XP. Ordinary RedFox Tow jobs do not yet advance the new shared RLS Recovery progression. Native Repo correctly bypasses RedFox reward handling so it cannot be double-paid.

Do not guess the paid-build Recovery reward identifier. Add this only after the exact 2.7.0.1 API/key is directly available again.

### Yard/property architecture

v0.5.0.23 already supports multiple yards, custom yard names, independent capacities, purchased-RLS-garage enumeration, facility positions, and RedFox-yard -> purchased-RLS-garage linking. The missing workflow was directly designating a purchased RLS property as a RedFox tow yard.

## v0.5.0.24 implemented change

Added `fleetTools.garage.designatePurchasedAsYard()` and Tow Yard Management controls.

A purchased RLS garage/recovery facility can now become an additional RedFox Tow & Recovery yard at its real physical facility location. The designation immediately links the new RedFox yard to the same native RLS garage ID, refuses duplicate facility designations, preserves existing yards, supports rename afterward, and rolls back if save fails.

This enables the new RLS Recovery Yard to become Yard 2/3/4 without replacing RedFox Yard 1, and also supports any other purchased player property that RLS exposes as a purchased garage facility.
