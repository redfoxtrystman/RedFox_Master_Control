# JOB-09 Audit — v0.5.0.35 RedFox Company Computer / Fleet Recovery

Date: 2026-08-22
Job: JOB-09 — RedFox Tow & Recovery Dispatch
Baseline: exact v0.5.0.34 package/source
Paid RLS reference: exact supplied RLS Career Overhaul 2.7.0.1 split archive, joined read-only for source verification

## User feedback driving this patch

- Native/custom RLS Tow business computer still would not close from its X even after matching Racing Team's BusinessManager/onMenuOpen pattern.
- A personally owned trailer was transferred into the Tow Company garage path, disappeared from the personal garage/world, and did not appear in the Tow business computer.

## Exact source-first findings

1. v0.5.0.34 company intake did **not delete** the contributed Career vehicle. `moveInventoryVehicle` preserves the same Career inventory record/ID, moves its `location` to the linked RLS garage, marks it `redfoxCompanyVehicle`, marks the Fleet Book unit as company-owned/assigned, removes the live world object, saves Tow state, and saves Career.
2. The stock RLS Business Computer vehicle tab reads `career_modules_business_businessInventory.getBusinessVehicles(businessId)`. Personally contributed Tow vehicles in v34 instead live in normal Career inventory + Fleet Book. Therefore the transferred trailer was invisible to the stock Tow business-computer Garage/Storage tab even though the Career vehicle record was preserved.
3. RLS Racing Team itself uses Business Inventory for its fleet, so copying Racing Team's open/close path did not solve the Tow architecture mismatch.
4. The existing RedFox Tow Vue storage route already closes reliably with `window.bngVue.gotoGameState("play")` and already knows how to build cards from Fleet Book, normal Career inventory, claimed vehicles, Tow Yard custody, and yard capacities.
5. The stock RLS Business Computer close button calls the RLS store teardown and `career_career.closeAllMenus()`. Because the custom RedFox business screen remained unreliable in runtime despite using the same open/close path, v35 stops using that stock screen as the normal Tow Company Computer.

## v0.5.0.35 behavior

- `OPEN TOW COMPANY COMPUTER` now opens the RedFox-owned Tow Company Computer route, not the stock RLS custom-business screen.
- The RedFox Tow Company Computer closes directly back to `play` using the proven RedFox Vue route behavior and leaves the Tow WEUI available.
- Company Fleet cards are built from the authoritative Fleet Book + normal Career inventory path used by personally contributed vehicles.
- Transferred trucks, trailers, race/sponsor cars, support vehicles, box trucks, and other personally contributed company vehicles therefore appear in the normal Tow Company Computer even though they are not RLS Business Inventory rows.
- Each fleet card now reports Career inventory ID, whether the Career record is present, and the native garage/location ID when available.
- Tow Yard custody/impound/abandoned records and company-owned/claimed disposition vehicles remain visible in separate sections of the same Tow Company Computer.
- Tow Yard storage/capacity summaries remain visible.
- Connected RLS Tow business bank name/balance are shown in the RedFox Tow Company Computer; the real RLS business account remains authoritative underneath it.
- The old stock RLS custom-business screen remains accessible only through the renamed `OPEN ADVANCED RLS STORAGE / FINANCES COMPUTER` control for backend diagnostics/legacy RLS Business Inventory work.
- Company-level Close now sends the UI to `play` directly and clears RedFox business context rather than depending on the unreliable stock custom-business X.
- One-step personally owned vehicle intake from v34 remains intact and preserves the same Career inventory identity with no purchase/sale money.
- No RLS source file was edited or replaced.

## Trailer recovery expectation

A trailer transferred by v34 should still exist in Career inventory at the linked Tow Yard garage location unless some separate runtime/save failure occurred. v35's RedFox Tow Company Computer should surface its Fleet Book/Career record. If the card reports `Career Record: MISSING — CHECK LOG`, stop and inspect the fresh log/save before attempting any additional transfer.
