# JOB-09 Audit — v0.5.0.34 RLS Racing-Computer Path / Easy Fleet Intake

Date: 2026-08-22
Job: JOB-09 — RedFox Tow & Recovery Dispatch
Baseline: exact v0.5.0.33 package/source
Paid RLS reference: exact supplied RLS Career Overhaul 2.7.0.1 split archive, reconstructed read-only before editing

## Owner feedback driving this patch

- The RedFox Tow business computer still would not close from its native X.
- The Tow computer should use the same open/close path as the RLS Racing Team business computer.
- Open Tow Company Computer / Close Tow Company Computer should be at the top of Tow Yard Management instead of buried inside an individual Tow Yard.
- Adding owned tow trucks/vehicles to the company garage is too complicated and should be a one-step action.

## Exact RLS source findings

1. `career/modules/business/racingTeam.lua` registers the Racing Team with `career_modules_business_businessManager.registerBusiness(...)` and registers an `onMenuOpen` callback with `registerBusinessCallback(...)`.
2. RLS Racing Team's `onMenuOpen` callback performs the `business-computer` route navigation. Normal business opening therefore runs through `businessManager.openBusinessMenu(businessType, businessId)` -> registered `onMenuOpen` callback -> business-computer route.
3. `BusinessComputerMain.vue` closes the normal business computer by resetting its store and calling `lua.career_career.closeAllMenus()`.
4. The exact paid RLS 2.7.0.1 override `overrides/career/career.lua` implements `closeAllMenus()` by navigating the UI router to `play`, with a `ChangeState` fallback for profile-route teardown.
5. v0.5.0.33 opened RedFox Tow by calling its business module `openMenu()` directly from WEUI. v0.5.0.34 removes that shortcut from the public path and asks RLS BusinessManager to open the business, matching Racing Team's callback flow.

## v0.5.0.34 behavior

- RedFox Tow's public business-computer open path now calls RLS `businessManager.openBusinessMenu("redfoxTow", businessId)`.
- The RedFox registered `onMenuOpen` callback performs the actual `business-computer` route navigation, avoiding recursive manager calls.
- Tow Company close uses the same `career_career.closeAllMenus()`/play route as RLS, with a narrow direct `ui_router.navigate("play")` fallback only if the Career close path cannot be called.
- Business-computer context is cleared after the company-level close request.
- Tow Yard Management now starts with a company-level `TOW COMPANY — COMPUTER & GARAGE` block before purchased facilities or individual yards.
- Top controls: Open Tow Company Computer, Close Tow Company Computer, Add Current Owned Vehicle to Tow Company Garage, Open Tow Company Garage / Edit / Paint / Parts.
- Open Company Computer auto-uses the primary yard, selected yard, first linked branch, or first current-map Tow Yard; if the chosen existing yard is not yet a RedFox business branch, opening the company computer links it in place first.
- One-step company vehicle intake accepts any verified personally owned Career vehicle type.
- Intake automatically registers an unregistered vehicle in Fleet Book, then chooses the selected linked Tow Yard, assigned linked Tow Yard, nearest linked Tow Yard, or first linked Tow Yard on the map.
- If the destination Tow Yard is not yet a RedFox business branch, intake links it before the garage transfer.
- Vehicle transfer still preserves the same Career/RLS inventory identity and exchanges no purchase/sale money.
- Existing Tow Yard Company Fleet / Shop Bay capacity remains authoritative.
- The older Fleet Book-only registration button remains available but is relabeled as an ADVANCED action.
- No RLS source files were edited or replaced.
