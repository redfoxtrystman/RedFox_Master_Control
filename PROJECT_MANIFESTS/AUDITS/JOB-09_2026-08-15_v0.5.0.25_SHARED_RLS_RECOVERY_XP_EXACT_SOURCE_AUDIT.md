## JOB-09 v0.5.0.25 implementation

All completed RedFox towing/recovery work now feeds `careerSkills-recovery`.

Standard Car Tow, Abandoned Vehicle Recovery and ordinary tow/impound work use the Repo-style 1x structure.

Roadside Rolled-Vehicle Recovery, Semi/Trailer Rollover Recovery and Roadway Accident Scene Cleanup/Multi-Car Crash use the Off-Road-style 2x structure.

For multi-target scenes, each recovered target contributes its skill-capped RLS-tier base XP. Distance XP accounts for the expected repeated tow distance.

### Completion, not later sale

Recovery XP is earned for doing the recovery. It is awarded when a paid RedFox job completes, an unpaid impound closes, refused delivery reaches Tow Yard custody, an abandoned recovery is completed/stored/disposed, a non-lienable recovered item is safely closed, or the paid-mission Force Complete failsafe is used.

Recovery XP is NOT attached to later salvage sales, valuables searches, direct Tow Yard vehicle sales, business vehicle sales, refunds or future FoxNet auction proceeds. This prevents the same recovery from generating tow XP again when its asset is monetized later.

## Duplicate protection

v0.5.0.25 adds a persistent `recoveryRewardLedger`, keyed by RedFox event ID, and also checks pending reward records. The ledger keeps the newest 750 entries. This prevents normal repeated completion/reload paths from paying Recovery XP twice for the same RedFox event.

Native Repo and native Off-Road Recovery never enter the RedFox reward ledger; RLS remains authoritative for both.

## Difficulty scaling

JOB-09 adds `careerSkills-recovery` to the reward table passed through RLS `career_modules_difficultyMode.scalePaymentRewardData(..., {includeMoney=false})`, matching native Repo/Off-Road behavior while leaving JOB-09's quoted Tow cash unchanged.
