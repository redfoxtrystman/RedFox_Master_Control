## Owner requirements driving this patch

The Tow business must become usable end-to-end before more feature expansion:

- Tow revenue belongs in the real RedFox Tow RLS business bank account, not only personal Career money.
- The existing RLS Bank must remain the place where the player transfers money Personal ↔ Tow Business.
- Any personally owned Career vehicle type may be contributed to the Tow Company with no sale/purchase payment: tow truck, trailer, race/sponsorship car, security vehicle, cargo/box truck, cement truck, or other vehicle.
- The contributed vehicle must remain the same owned Career vehicle/inventory identity rather than being deleted and replaced by a duplicate.
- Tow Company vehicles must remain editable through a normal Career garage workflow for pictures, parts/upgrades, paint, and any customization tools the normal garage supports.
- Paid RedFox Tow Yard Company Fleet / Shop Bay capacity is authoritative logical company storage and must not be defeated merely because the linked property has fewer literal parking positions.
- Abandoned recovery should automatically enter Tow Yard custody after successful delivery instead of requiring the detached Keep/Sell decision for the normal case.
- Disposition holds must use real elapsed hours because the owner's current Career save has frozen/non-advancing game-day time.
- A one-vehicle development override is required so disposition state can be tested immediately.
- Hidden backend state must be visible in diagnostics.

## Exact RLS source findings used

### RLS Bank
`lua/ge/extensions/career/modules/bank.lua`

Verified public functions:
- `createBusinessAccount(businessType, businessId, businessName)`
- `getBusinessAccount(businessType, businessId)`
- `getAccountBalance(accountId)`
- `getAccountTransactions(accountId, limit)`

RLS's native Bank UI can therefore handle Personal ↔ RedFox Tow business transfers once the formal `redfoxTow / redfox_tow_company` account exists. Business-originated account-to-account transfers use RLS's existing pending-transfer behavior; JOB-09 does not replace it.

### Existing JOB-09 owned-vehicle move path
The v0.5.0.32 source already had the safe same-identity path through Career inventory:
- resolves current live vehicle to the real Career inventory ID;
- calls `career_modules_inventory.moveVehicleToGarage(...)` where possible;
- verifies the same inventory record now points at the linked purchased RLS garage;
- preserves ownership and inventory identity;
- stores the previous personal garage for reversal;
- does not create a duplicate vehicle.

### Normal Career garage editor
JOB-09 already had a verified compatibility path using `career_modules_inventory.openMenuFromComputer(garageId)`. v0.5.0.33 exposes that path directly for the Tow Company fleet so the linked real Career garage is the current editor/picture/paint/parts surface.

### RLS Business Computer distinction
The RLS `BusinessVehiclesTab.vue` is backed by RLS Business Inventory and its Pull Out/Put Away actions call that inventory directly. Existing JOB-09 Company Fleet vehicles are normal Career inventory vehicles moved to the Tow Yard's linked real garage, not RLS Business Inventory rows. Faking those rows in the RLS Business Computer would make its actions target nonexistent business-inventory IDs. Therefore v0.5.0.33 does **not** fake Company Fleet vehicles into that tab. The tab is renamed `RLS Storage` to make this distinction visible; the actual current Company Fleet garage is opened through the normal Career garage.

## v0.5.0.33 behavior

### 1. Tow job money → real RLS Tow business account
JOB-09 queued cash now attempts `career_modules_bank.addFunds()` against the formal RedFox Tow business account first.

Recovery XP still uses the shared `careerSkills-recovery` reward path and remains separate from cash.

The payment queue now persists independent settlement flags for cash and Recovery XP. This prevents a partial failure from paying one component twice on the next retry.

If an already-purchased RedFox Tow business is missing its bank account, JOB-09 attempts the existing RedFox/RLS business-account repair path without triggering a business-purchase prompt.

If no formal Tow business account is available at all, existing personal-payment fallback remains as a loss-prevention fallback; diagnostics make this visible.

Direct Tow business expenses/refunds that use `M.business.adjustMoney()` now prefer the real Tow business account when it exists. This includes the existing Tow business-side storage/title/acquisition workflows that call this function.

### 2. Native RLS Bank remains authoritative for transfers
No second fake bank-transfer system is created.

The normal RLS Bank can transfer Personal ↔ Tow Business using its own account/transfer rules because `business_redfoxTow_redfox_tow_company` is a real RLS business account.

### 3. Any personally owned vehicle → Tow Company
Company Fleet adds `TRANSFER CURRENT OWNED VEHICLE TO TOW COMPANY`.

Rules:
- current vehicle must resolve to a real owned Career inventory record;
- any vehicle type is allowed;
- no purchase/sale money changes hands;
- existing Fleet Book record is reused when linked by inventory ID, otherwise one is created;
- same Career inventory ID is preserved;
- nearest/assigned RedFox Tow Yard is selected;
- that yard must currently be linked to a real purchased RLS garage/property;
- previous personal garage is retained for return;
- transfer is verified after movement.

Additional role labels now include Race / Sponsorship, Security / Patrol, Cargo / Shipping, Company Utility, and Other in addition to towing/recovery roles.

### 4. Tow Company logical storage is authoritative
RedFox `Company Fleet / Shop Bays` remains the company storage capacity.

If RedFox still has a paid logical company slot but the linked RLS property's native physical garage reports no free literal garage slot, JOB-09 uses its existing verified full-garage assignment path to keep the same owned Career vehicle assigned to the linked Tow garage. This separates logical stored-vehicle capacity from physical parking positions.

This is intended to allow, for example, six company vehicles in an upgraded eight-bay RedFox Tow Yard even if the source RLS property exposes fewer physical parking locations. Physical world spawning/parking remains a separate later system.

### 5. Real Tow Company garage/editor access
Company Fleet adds:
`OPEN TOW COMPANY GARAGE — VEHICLES / EDIT / PAINT / PARTS`

It opens the normal Career garage at the linked RedFox Tow Yard property. This is the current supported surface for normal vehicle thumbnails/details and normal Career vehicle editing, paint, parts/upgrades, and whatever decal/customization capability that normal garage itself exposes.

The RLS Business Computer's previous `Garage` label is renamed `RLS Storage`; it remains only the earlier RLS Business Inventory prototype view and is not falsely populated with Career Company Fleet rows.

**Native Career Sell is not yet rerouted for contributed Company Fleet vehicles.** The WEUI displays an explicit warning not to use the native Sell action on a company vehicle in this version. Company-owned sale routing to the Tow business account remains a required follow-up.

### 6. Real-hour impound/disposition timers
Normal lien/disposition eligibility no longer depends on Career simulation days.

New default: 6 real elapsed hours.
Settings cycle: 3h / 6h / 12h / 24h.

Timer basis is stored with:
- `storedAtRealTime`
- `holdHours`
- `eligibleAtRealTime`
- `holdTimerBasis = real_hours`

Older impound records migrate from their existing real `storedAt` timestamp. Old Career-day fields are no longer read for eligibility.

Storage billing remains a per-24-real-hours rate.

### 7. Development disposition override
Settings → Payment / Impound Testing now supports:
`DEV OVERRIDE — MAKE SELECTED VEHICLE ELIGIBLE NOW`

The user first opens/expands one Tow Yard custody vehicle, then opens Settings. The override applies only to that selected impound-hold record and persists the new eligibility state.

### 8. Automatic abandoned storage
After a successful abandoned-vehicle delivery, JOB-09 now attempts to place the vehicle directly into that exact Tow Yard's custody/abandoned hold.

Normal success no longer stops at the detached Keep/Sell decision.

If automatic storage fails, the active call is deliberately left in the existing manual decision state as a recovery/failsafe instead of deleting the vehicle or closing the call.

### 9. Business diagnostics
Settings now shows:
- Tow business bank connected/account ID;
- bank transfer API availability;
- add-funds availability;
- remove-funds availability;
- current Tow business balance;
- native Career garage editor availability;
- Company Fleet unit count.

### 10. Business-computer close fallback
The native RLS Business Computer X remains an unresolved external/UI close-path issue in testing.

v0.5.0.33 adds a temporary Tow Yard Management button:
`FORCE CLOSE RLS BUSINESS COMPUTER`

It calls the exact `career_career.closeAllMenus()` Lua path used by RLS's own `exitBusinessComputerToPlay` flow. It intentionally does not close the RedFox Tow WEUI. This is a fallback, not a claim that the native X itself is repaired.
