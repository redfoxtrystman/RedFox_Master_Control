- Claim converts the exact custody record into the same exact yard's virtual company/shop inventory.
- No personal purchased garage is required for claim.

- An interrupted pending claim is reconciled from the business ledger.
- Legacy half-finished claims are blocked for manual review instead of deleting or duplicating a Career vehicle.
- Personal/RLS garage transfer remains a separate explicit action.

### Active-call freeze/stutter repair

The runtime evidence showed repeated freezes only while a call was active and immediate relief after drop-off. Source review found two recurring active-call operations matching that pattern:

1. route/marker path rebuilding on an eight-second cycle; and
2. a full active-job scene snapshot plus JSON write every ten seconds.

Repairs:

- route/marker rebuilding is now event/movement-driven;
- pickup routes refresh only after a configurable interval and meaningful target movement;
- delivery route is set on the pickup-to-delivery phase transition rather than rebuilt periodically;
- manual Route buttons still force a refresh;
- default active-job checkpoint interval changes from 10 to 30 seconds;
- an untouched saved 10-second setting migrates to 30 seconds through settings schema 14;
- checkpoints enforce a 15-second absolute minimum;
- repeated identical snapshots are fingerprinted and skipped;
- periodic checkpoint logging is suppressed;
- player-truck checkpointing no longer scans every Career inventory object;
- known support identity metadata is reused instead of re-resolved each checkpoint;
- snapshot construction is protected and cannot leave the save lock permanently engaged;
- completed Random Events scenes without a required live tick no longer enter the bridge update every frame.

These are static/source findings. David's runtime test remains required to prove the freeze is resolved.

### Emergency vehicle rules

- Added expanded identification for police, sheriff, patrol, fire department, fire service, fire brigade, ambulance, EMS and related emergency configurations.
- Strong emergency metadata overrides an accidental broad `heavy_target` classification for abandoned/private-lien safety.
- Police, fire and EMS cannot enter:
  - abandoned vehicle calls;
  - private payer-default acquisition;
  - ordinary abandoned-title/lien flows.
- Emergency vehicles remain available for appropriate breakdown, accident, rollover and agency recovery work.
- Emergency target selection is separated from civilian pools and limited by call-type probability:
  - ordinary tow default: 6%;
  - recovery default: 10%;
  - accident default: 12%.
- One emergency target starts an eight-selection cooldown, preventing a repeated police/fire/EMS streak.
- Emergency target jobs are forced to government/emergency-services agency payment and cannot randomly become private unpaid/lien vehicles.
- A rare emergency selection with no matching installed configuration falls back to a civilian; civilian pool exhaustion never forces repeated emergency vehicles.
- Saved civilian scene templates exclude emergency replacements unless the saved target itself is an emergency configuration.
