## Company Fleet Garage implementation

New internal/public behaviors:

- find company records by Fleet unit or company record ID;
- identify the current Career inventory ID through `career_modules_inventory.getInventoryIdFromVehicleId`;
- update part conditions before transfer when available;
- capture Career inventory configuration and part-condition data;
- save RedFox company record before removing the Career inventory entry;
- remove through `career_modules_inventory.removeVehicle`;
- verify removal through `career_modules_inventory.getVehicle`;
- block retrieval when removal is not confirmed;
- spawn a confirmed company truck at its assigned RedFox tow yard;
- return it to virtual company storage;
- force-return retrieved company vehicles during mission end or extension unload;
- expose:
  - `getCompanyGarage()`
  - `transferCurrentFleetUnitToCompanyGarage(unitId)`
  - `retrieveCompanyVehicle(recordId)`
  - `returnCompanyVehicle(recordId)`

Diagnostics:

- `[RedFox][TOW][COMPANY_TRANSFER_AUDIT]`
- `[RedFox][TOW][COMPANY_RETRIEVE]`
- `[RedFox][TOW][COMPANY_RETURN]`
