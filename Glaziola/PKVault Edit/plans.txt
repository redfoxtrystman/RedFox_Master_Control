PKVAULT EDIT — PLANS AND CONTINUATION NOTES
Updated: 2026-10-13
Existing project: .NET 10 Pokémon storage / save editor desktop app, NOT a Cobblemon mod.
Known solution/modules: pkvault.sln, PKVault.Core, PKVault.Desktop, frontend.
Previously used GitHub branch: pkvault-v8-uranium-insurgence in redfoxtrystman/RedFox_Master_Control. Verify actual current branch/head and code before development.
Status: Existing project. Plans and regressions below may already be implemented in some alpha builds.

PURPOSE
Cross-generation Pokémon storage, save compatibility, item/inventory support, trading, Pokedex, quests and a discovery/progression-based Shop, with extremely careful preservation of user save data.

1. CORE STORAGE / SAFETY
- Party, PC and item moving must be reliable and atomic; never erase real Pokémon, spawn phantom Pokémon or corrupt inventories.
- Maintain safe backups, rollback, proper save transitions, unique item IDs, left/right independent panels and same-save open support.
- Resizable item boxes up to 300 slots and renameable item boxes.
- Gen 1 special-glitch handling: show legitimate MissingNo/'M records but block bad phantom placeholders and unsafe writes/sorts.
- Do not regress legacy game storage or overwrite unsupported Pokémon data.

2. GAME / SAVE SUPPORT
- Official Pokémon game generations and appropriate version/region/form restrictions; Pokedex and sprite rendering.
- Pokémon Uranium and Insurgence support with valid save and Pokémon/item data round trips, including Ruby Marshal .rxdata, correct species/moves/forms, party/PC, bags and game-specific items.
- Protect TMs/HMs/Key Items and prevent invalid cross-game export.
- TMT/other ROM hack support should preserve unknown/expanded raw species, types and moves during round-trips, using provenance/profile metadata and safe vanilla export guards.
- Uranium Tandor Dex including all 200 entries; appropriate seen/caught and regional sorting; special sprites such as Aotius/Mutios.
- Prevent uncaught/locked custom Pokémon from leaking their names in hover UI.

3. SHOP (PRIORITY) / ITEM BANK
- Shop unlocks stock based on quests, discoveries, prior item acquisition, generation/game progression.
- Example: committed Eevee -> Flareon evolution unlocks Fire Stone purchase; evolution items unlock after first relevant obtain when intended.
- Rarity/progression-appropriate buy/sell costs; useful recovery/rebuy mechanics; honest item cost display.
- Item inventory categories, berries, valuables, TMs, evolution stones, correct sprites/icon sizes and inventory/Shop/Quests visibility toggles (default all on).
- Item boxes, splitting, drag/drop, bank quantities and HGSS Apricorn withdrawal must work.
- EXPEDITIONS were previously SHELVED; they belong in a possible.txt backlog if revisited, NOT current Shop work.

4. EVOLUTION
- Item evolutions consume an owned item and happen immediately, just like the existing trade evolution workflow.
- Do NOT introduce a new pending Save/Cancel interaction exclusively for item evolution.
- Correct game, generation, species, gender, region and form restrictions; preserve linked-version sync and reward progress.

5. TRADING
- Integrated Pokémon trading from storage with 0-6 Pokémon offered, including unequal trades or gifts; safe transactional reservation, confirmation, rollback and refresh.
- Direct IP, Radmin/Hamachi and protocol/version checks.
- Incoming Pokémon must enter the RECIPIENT'S CHOSEN BOX, not a random/default one.
- Update Pokédex for eligible official received Pokémon; exclude invalid glitch records, Eggs and placeholder species 0.
- Preserve trainer details and other Pokémon attributes.

6. QUESTS / REWARDS / POKEDEX
- Accurate Seen/Caught tracking, cross-save achievements, dailies, contracts and one-time rewards.
- Prior modest playtime reward proposals: 15 minutes = ₽2,000; 2 hours = ₽10,000; 10 hours = ₽25,000.
- Prevent repeated reward exploits; keep reward persistence consistent with existing save-confirmation rules.
- Avoid ROM patching/hacks to implement rewards.

7. OUTSTANDING BUGS / QA
- Fix or verify 'pkvault-db already open' and SQLite concurrency/save errors, undisposed database context, and file sharing races.
- Red PC-to-PC item deletion, false phantom items and vanishing transferred items.
- HG/SS Apricorn withdrawal and achievement accounting.
- Money/meta-key corruption, changing item-box names, unsupported items or bad bag mapping.
- Mis-sized category icons, item sprites and Gen 1 Elixir asset alias problems.
- Save/modify flow must not clear party/boxes, and user data must remain safe when changing saves.
- Re-test prior alpha53 series bugfixes on CURRENT source; do not assume all claims were validated.

KEEP SEPARATE
PKVault is its own desktop app, NOT related to Cobblemon Mystery Dungeon or Pixelmon Rejuvenated except that users may enjoy similar collection features. Existing software should never be replaced with a placeholder implementation.
