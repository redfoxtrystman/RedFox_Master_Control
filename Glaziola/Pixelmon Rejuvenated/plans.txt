PIXELMON REJUVENATED — MASTER PLANS
Updated: 2026-10-13 | Minecraft 1.21.1 NeoForge | Cobblemon addon
Status: PLANNING ONLY. Entirely separate from Cobblemon Mystery Dungeon.

GOAL: Recreate Pixelmon's distinctive gameplay mechanics, special variants, environments, and structures in Cobblemon style, reusing existing Cobblemon and addon Pokémon species wherever possible.

1. SPIRIT POKÉMON AND GRAVEYARDS
- Add Spirit Eevee and all eight Eeveelutions with Cobblemon-style models, textures and permanent aspects.
- Reproduce Graveyard A/B/C and Haunted Graveyard A/B appearances, locations and appropriate Pokémon encounters as closely as possible.
- Special rule: Spirit Eeveelutions retain their usual primary type and GAIN Ghost as secondary type. Eevee Normal/Ghost; Vaporeon Water/Ghost; Jolteon Electric/Ghost; Flareon Fire/Ghost; Espeon Psychic/Ghost; Umbreon Dark/Ghost; Leafeon Grass/Ghost; Glaceon Ice/Ghost; Sylveon Fairy/Ghost.
- Spirit appearance persists through evolution, trade and PC storage.

2. ULTRA SPACE
- Recreate Ultra Space dimension: floating terrain, six Ultra biomes (Deep Sea, Desert, Forest, Jungle, Plant, Crater), wormholes, low gravity, fog, structures, world return, rare encounters, Shiny/Hidden Ability modifiers.
- Reproduce Pixelmon's FULL spawn lists (ordinary Pokémon plus Ultra Beasts), levels and encounter conditions, not just a generic Beast spawn pool.
- Ultra Beasts include Nihilego, Pheromosa, Guzzlord, Stakataka, Kartana, Blacephalon, Buzzwole, Poipole, Xurkitree, Celesteela; Naganadel evolved from Poipole. Confirm spawn mappings against the chosen reference release.

3. DAYCARE / BREEDING
- New Daycare block, GUI and two breeding Pokémon slots.
- REUSE Cobblemon's Pasture roaming system: visually display both Pokémon wandering around the Daycare, fenced roaming and native animations.
- Breeding compatibility, Ditto/Egg Groups, relevant IV/Nature/ability/move/form inheritance, progression, Eggs.
- Eggs occupy PARTY slots and hatch through walking steps, not as standard inventory items. PC-stored Eggs do not gain steps. Safe persistence and retrieval.

4. CLASSIC APRICORNS
- Old Pixelmon short/bush-like Apricorn plants (before modern trees), all seven original colors; natural generation, growth stages, right-click harvest, replant.
- Produce Cobblemon-compatible Apricorns. Optional config to coexist with or replace natural Apricorn trees.

5. SPECIAL SKINS AND VARIANTS
- Target at least 90% of Pixelmon's individual special appearances, converted into Cobblemon art style.
- Examples: Spirit, Ashen, Alter, Strike, Pink, Valencian, Drowned, Zombie, Rainbow, Valentine, Christmas/Elf, Witch/Halloween, Summer, Crystal Steelix, Alien Celebi, special Arbok forms.
- Accurate acquisition/encounter methods where practical, with variant-aware evolution, trading and PC support.

6. LEGENDARY/MYTHICAL ACQUISITION

- Legendary spawning, rituals, shrines, structures, rewards and animations as in chosen Pixelmon version.
- Use existing Pokémon from Cobblemon, Myths and Legends or compatible addons where available; NEVER register duplicate species.

7. DROWNED WORLD
- Additional Plane of Water/Drowned World dimension with Warm, Crimson and Dark environments, Drowned Pokémon/skins, quest mechanics, underwater structures, and Drowned Lugia.

8. PIXELMON BOSSES

13. LEGENDARY/MYTHICAL SPAWN ANNOUNCEMENTS AND DESPAWN PROTECTION (FEATURE #16)
- Recreate Pixelmon's separate, configurable legendary/mythical spawn-attempt system instead of handling them as ordinary wild Pokemon spawns.
- Broadcast a server-wide chat message with the legendary/mythical species and actual biome (example: "A legendary Mew has spawned in a Jungle biome!"). Biome only by default: no coordinates unless an optional config enables them.
- Respect the eligible Pokémon's original encounter rules, time, weather, biome and rarity. Prefer existing Pokémon species supplied by Cobblemon / Myths and Legends and prevent competing addon spawn logic from creating duplicates.
- Set a CONFIGURABLE 15-MINUTE REAL-WORLD lifespan for natural legendary spawns by default, as requested. For reference, Pixelmon documentation lists a 6,000-tick (5-minute) default, so 15 minutes is an intentional change.
- Do NOT allow ordinary Cobblemon distance-based despawning, entity cap cleanup or routine wild Pokemon cleanup to remove the encounter early.
- Track each spawned encounter server-side with persistent identity, biome, location, dimension, spawn timestamp and expiration; manage loaded/unloaded chunks safely to avoid duplicate spawns.
- Ideally preserve the same encounter if the chunk unloads/reloads, or reinstantiate it safely only if needed. No mandatory permanent chunk force-loading. Optional short-lived force-load tickets may be configurable but must release on capture, defeat, timeout or cleanup.
- Expire uncaught encounters after the configured wall-clock time, even if the chunk was unloaded, with no hidden respawns or item duplication. Clear tracking on successful capture and define battle interaction with expiry to avoid despawning in the middle of a legitimate battle.
- Offer settings for announcements, natural-spawn enablement, interval, attempt chance, despawn minutes, whether mythicals participate, simultaneous cap, and optional position/coordinates display.
- This applies to NATURALLY SPAWNED legendaries/mythicals; do not accidentally time-limit player-owned Pokémon or ritual/shrine summons unless separately configured.
- PIXELMON reference documented defaults to compare: legendary-spawn-ticks=25000 average with 60%-140% variation, legendary-spawn-chance about 0.30, legendary-despawn-ticks=6000, display-legendary-global-message=true. Verify values for selected Pixelmon release before coding.
