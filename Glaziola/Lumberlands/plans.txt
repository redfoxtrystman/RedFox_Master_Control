LUMBERLANDS — PLANS AND CONTINUATION NOTES
Updated: 2026-10-13
Project: Existing Minecraft 1.21.1 FABRIC mod based on Roblox Lumber Tycoon 2 (LT2)
Status: Existing development project. Items below include requirements and outstanding QA; they are not all confirmed implemented.

GOAL: Reproduce LT2's trees, wood types, branches, foliage, chopping, tree growth, draggable logs and realistic/accurate tree physics in Minecraft. Prefer verified LT2 models and materials over fabricated lookalikes.

1. LT2 TREE TYPES / SHAPES
- Reproduce authentic tree branching, taper, trunk proportions, stump shapes, leaf arrangements and species-specific growth, with realistic procedural variation matching LT2.
- LT2's Generic tree corresponds to Oak; LT2's tree called Oak corresponds to Elm. Do not confuse these naming schemes.
- Cave Crawler must have irregular stump splitting and branch structure, with multiple Foil leaf pieces rather than a simplistic fixed model. Longer stumps can occur.
- Frost/Ice trees need verified LT2-accurate growth appearance (prior approximations were inaccurate). Verify against source/wiki/reference images.
- Distinctive tree/wood types include Neon, Sinister/Spooky, Blue Spruce, End Times and Lone Tree; validate unique behavior per species.
- Leaves need LT2-like growth, scaling and post-felling/falling death behavior.

2. MODELS / MATERIALS / RENDERING
- Use actual LT2-style geometry and reference-accurate materials where permissions permit. Prior user provided Roblox reference game files; source data should be examined rather than guessed.
- Reproduce correct pre-2022 Roblox material maps, tiling and BrickColor tint, instead of flat-color placeholders.
- Sinister/Spooky should exhibit appropriate black granite/marble-style look, not incorrect flat purple/black.
- End Times blue glow spots and foliage placement must track the real geometry.
- Chopped pieces expose accurate internal wood surfaces.
- Neon material must remain compatible with Sodium + Iris and should not cause red tint or shader breakage.

3. TREE PHYSICS / TREE CONTACT
- Falling trees, logs and separated segments have physically accurate collision matching their visible models.
- Trees should be solid/walkable at the physical wood surface, and drop/fall appropriately with the player on them.
- On chop, collision shape must update immediately: no invisible lingering solid tree hitbox.
- Fix small-piece and trunk jitter/bouncing on contact with terrain; prevent penetration and repeated upward correction, unstable sleeping and runaway forces.
- Handle grabbing, dragging, mass/inertia/friction, sleeping, multi-contact physics and splitting without server TPS or client stability problems.
- Dedicated server-authoritative tree data/body graph and efficient pose syncing; adaptive simulation bounds as needed.
- Consider prior inspiration from Create Aeronautics physics when appropriate, but do not assume it provides a copyable drop-in system.

4. AXES AND CHOPPING
- Recreate LT2's standard, special, limited and unobtainable hatchets with actual 3D models/textures, damage, range, cooldown and specialist advantages.
- Prior development notes describe 32 axe types: 8 standard, 20 special/limited, 4 unobtainable; verify current registry.
- Ensure correct Minecraft first-person attack swing and LT2-like third-person multi-height chopping feedback.
- Add/maintain LT2 grab dot UI/interaction for lifting wood/tree pieces.
- Test chopping of branches, segmentation and precise interior cut rendering.

5. CREATIVE ITEMS / DEVELOPMENT TOOLS
- Include tree seeds in a Lumberlands creative tab.
- Add configurable developer tree-growth-speed multiplier.
- Remove unintended magma-tree log/block content.
- Ensure trees, wood pieces and interactions persist across unload/reload and multiplayer.

6. KNOWN ISSUES TO RECHECK IN LATEST BUILD
- Tree red tint and non-LT2 materials/shape fidelity.
- Inaccurate hitboxes, missing tree solidity, invisible hitboxes after chopping and player floating over walking surfaces.
- Severe physics jitter, especially tiny wood pieces, including clipping below the ground.
- Incorrect leaf death after felling; faulty Frost growth; Cave Crawler morphology.
- Hatchet missing or corrupted textures and shader compatibility.
- Prior reported startup failure for EntityGetterTreeCollisionMixin involving interface field LUMBERLANDS_TREE_QUERY_PAD (verify whether still present before fixing).
- Test under actual Fabric + Sodium + Iris, not just compilation.
